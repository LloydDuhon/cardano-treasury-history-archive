# Governance

This repository supports Cardano treasury history analysis for Budget Committee
work. Governance context is provided by the Budget Committee documentation:

https://budgetcommittee.docs.intersectmbo.org/

## Maintainer

Current maintainer:

Lloyd Duhon <lloyd.duhon@intersectmbo.org>

The maintainer is responsible for:

- Reviewing pull requests.
- Maintaining source provenance standards.
- Deciding when low-confidence data can be promoted or must remain flagged.
- Coordinating security fixes.
- Preserving a reproducible path from raw source snapshots to reports and site
  artifacts.

## Contribution Review

Contributions are welcome. Data changes require provenance and conservative
confidence labels. Schema, workflow, ETL, generated report, and published-site
changes require maintainer review.

Where a change affects Budget Committee interpretation or public reporting, the
maintainer may defer final acceptance until Budget Committee review.

## Human Review And AI Assistance

AI-assisted rows in reports are triage outputs unless explicitly marked as
human-reviewed. Human review remains the authority for adjudicating disputed
matches, proposer relationships, and work-overlap conclusions.
