03 / METHOD

A present-day lens, not a historical claim.

Vision 2030 did not govern the earlier funds. This report assigns one primary pillar per proposal to prevent double counting. Specific challenge names drive high-confidence mappings; open categories use conservative phrase scoring; unresolved cases remain visible as ambiguous.

Delivered means an officially funded proposal whose archived project status is complete. The associated amount is its recorded award value, not audited expenditure. It is documented completion, not an independent impact assessment. Evidence is weakest for Funds 2–5, partial for Funds 6–9, milestone-based from Fund 10 onward, and still accruing for recent funds.
